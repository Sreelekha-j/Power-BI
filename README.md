🛍️ DMart Sales & Operational Intelligence Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that shows how DMart is doing: its sales, customers, products, deliveries and payments.

📌 About the Project

DMart wanted to make better decisions using its sales data. I analyzed 25,000 orders and built an interactive dashboard to answer questions like:

How are sales changing month by month and year by year?
Which product types (Local, Branded, Imported) bring the most revenue?
Who are the customers, by age and gender?
Which shipping modes are used most?
How many orders get cancelled, and why?
How do customers prefer to pay?
📂 Dataset
25,000 orders from January 2021 to December 2023
29 columns, including customer age and gender, product, price, discount, state, clicks, time on website, shipping, order status and payment details
🛠 Tools Used
Power BI: to build the charts and the dashboard
Power Query: to clean the data
DAX: to create new columns and measures like revenue, cancellation rate and average order value
AI visuals: Key Influencers and Decomposition Tree, to find what drives cancellations and revenue
Slicers: so users can filter by state, category and month
🔄 Steps Followed
Loaded the Excel data into Power BI
Cleaned the data
Created new columns and measures with DAX
Built the charts
Combined everything into a 3-page dashboard
📊 Dashboard Pages
Page	What it shows
Executive Overview	Main numbers (revenue, orders, average order value, cancellation rate), sales trend, sales by state
Detailed View	Customers by age and gender, ad-to-purchase funnel, discount vs. sales, top products, ratings
AI Visual & Conclusion	What affects cancellations, a revenue breakdown, and the final summary

💡 Key Findings
Total revenue: ₹1.69 Cr from 25,000 orders
Average order value: about ₹709
Local products bring in about half of all revenue
All four states have similar sales, with Maharashtra slightly ahead
57% of customers are male and 43% are female
Free shipping is the most used shipping mode
Debit cards are the most used payment method. Cash on Delivery is used the least.
About 7% of orders are cancelled and about 5% are returned
✅ Suggestions
Stock more Local products, since they sell the most
Look into why orders get cancelled and fix the main causes
Offer rewards for online payments to make paying easier and faster
Use targeted offers to attract more female customers
