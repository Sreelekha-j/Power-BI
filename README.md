🛍️ DMart Sales & Operational Intelligence Dashboard<br><br>

A Power BI dashboard that shows how DMart is doing: its sales, customers, products, deliveries and payments.<br><br>

📌 About the Project<br>

DMart wanted to make better decisions using its sales data. I analyzed 25,000 orders and built an interactive dashboard to answer questions like:<br>

How are sales changing month by month and year by year?<br>
Which product types (Local, Branded, Imported) bring the most revenue?<br>
Who are the customers, by age and gender?<br>
Which shipping modes are used most?<br>
How many orders get cancelled, and why?<br>
How do customers prefer to pay?<br><br>

📂 Dataset<br>

25,000 orders from January 2021 to December 2023<br>
29 columns, including customer age and gender, product, price, discount, state, clicks, time on website, shipping, order status and payment details<br><br>

🛠 Tools Used<br>

Power BI: to build the charts and the dashboard<br>
Power Query: to clean the data<br>
DAX: to create new columns and measures like revenue, cancellation rate and average order value<br>
AI visuals: Key Influencers and Decomposition Tree, to find what drives cancellations and revenue<br>
Slicers: so users can filter by state, category and month<br><br>

🔄 Steps Followed<br>

Loaded the Excel data into Power BI<br>
Cleaned the data<br>
Created new columns and measures with DAX<br>
Built the charts<br>
Combined everything into a 3-page dashboard<br><br>

📊 Dashboard Pages<br>

Page	What it shows<br>
Executive Overview	Main numbers (revenue, orders, average order value, cancellation rate), sales trend, sales by state<br>
Detailed View	Customers by age and gender, ad-to-purchase funnel, discount vs. sales, top products, ratings<br>
AI Visual & Conclusion	What affects cancellations, a revenue breakdown, and the final summary<br><br>

💡 Key Findings<br>

Total revenue: ₹1.69 Cr from 25,000 orders<br>
Average order value: about ₹709<br>
Local products bring in about half of all revenue<br>
All four states have similar sales, with Maharashtra slightly ahead<br>
57% of customers are male and 43% are female<br>
Free shipping is the most used shipping mode<br>
Debit cards are the most used payment method. Cash on Delivery is used the least.<br>
About 7% of orders are cancelled and about 5% are returned<br><br>

✅ Suggestions<br>
Stock more Local products, since they sell the most<br>
Look into why orders get cancelled and fix the main causes<br>
Offer rewards for online payments to make paying easier and faster<br>
Use targeted offers to attract more female customers<br><br>
